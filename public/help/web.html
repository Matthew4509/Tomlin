Web: searching and reading pages

The Web switch, the search engine, and the pace it reads at.

Beside Quick and Think, each chat has Web: off. Press it (Web: on) and the model may search the web and read pages while it answers: it searches, opens the results that look best and answers from them, and the answer's place says what it is doing ("Searching the web", "Reading example.com"). Under the answer, Web: says what it searched for and which pages it read.

Your search words go to the search engine chosen in Settings, Data and lock, Web search (DuckDuckGo unless you change it; Brave Search with your own key, or a SearXNG of your own); the pages come straight to this PC, and PDFs are read too.

A page is opened only at an address given in the chat (a search result, a link on a page it read, or one you wrote), so words on a page cannot make the model send your chat to an address it makes up; a page on this PC or your home network is never opened.

It reads at about a person's pace, so a site's firewall is far less likely to take it for an attack and ban your home address (every PC at home shares one, and each PC keeps its own pace): 5 to 7 seconds between pages on the same site (other sites at once), 10 seconds between searches, at most 3 pages at a time and 8 for one answer. The answer's place says when it is waiting ("Waiting 6 s before the next page on github.com").

A site that says it is busy or asks to slow down is waited for once, as long as it asks up to a minute; if it still says no, it is left alone for the rest of that answer.

It works with models on this PC whose chat format has tools (Qwen 3 and 3.5 have them; a model without says so under the answer). Small models search less well than bigger ones. Each page read takes room in the model's context, so a bigger context (the gear beside its Connect button) lets it read more.
