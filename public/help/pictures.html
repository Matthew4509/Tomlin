Getting a good picture

Draft, Mid or High, people, icons, cartoons and weak spots.

  • The Draw form: type what to draw, then pick a Shape (Auto, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9 or 9:16: the picture keeps the size this model is made for, in that shape) and a Style (Auto picks one from your words; Photo, Flat for icons and graphics, or Cartoon). Leave out holds words the picture should not have: type a word and press Enter or +, and × takes one away. The words TOMLIN adds by itself are shown too, with dashed edges and where they come from (the artist's way of working, the style, a portrait, the model); × on one of those stops it being left out, and "Put back" brings them all back.
  • Advanced: closed unless you open it. It has an exact width and height (instead of Shape) and how to fit them, the seed (turn off "New seed each time" to draw the same picture again), steps, strength (CFG), the method, Custom style (no style words added), icon sizes with an .ico, and Add a list to the queue. When it is closed and something in it is set, its line says what, for example "Advanced: seed 1234, 30 steps". "Reset to defaults" puts it all back to Auto. The form is kept as you left it for each artist in Draw as, and for this PC's own pane, in this browser.
  • Photo for a blog: Style "Photo" with Realistic Vision. Describe it like a photo: "a reading chair in a sunlit living room, 35mm photo, natural light". For a wide picture, pick Shape 4:3 or 16:9.
  • Draft, Mid or High: beside Draw. Draft is small and quick, with several to pick from: press "Full quality" on the one you like (same seed, sharper hands, keys and skin). Mid is the size the model is made for. High is its largest; with the Sharpener for High (under Models, Helpers) and pictures on a graphics card, it is enlarged to twice the size after it is drawn (on a CPU the sharpener is far too slow, so it is not used). Each button shows the size you get.
  • Two or more people: with no size given, the picture is drawn wide so both fit. A request about people is never turned into an icon by itself; the Flat style cuts the background away, and the picture says so.
  • Cartoons and cartoon profile pictures: use DreamShaper, Style "Cartoon" (or "cartoon" in the prompt). "cartoon profile picture of a friendly baker" draws a square head-and-shoulders picture; then press "Set as profile photo" and pick whose it is. DreamShaper sometimes draws a ring round the head for these.
  • Icons: use DreamShaper. A photo model in the Flat style reads "plain white background" as a studio photo shoot and often adds a person. With a photo model, try "product photo, isolated object, light grey background".
  • People: say who, with an age ("a woman in her 30s"); left out, the model tends to draw someone young. For no people, put "people, person" in Leave out and use Mid or High: Drafts on the fast models (CFG 1) ignore it, and "no people" in the prompt itself can add one.
  • Weak spots: hands, close-ups of keyboards, and any text or signs. Wide shots come out best.
  • Pictures and Character sets: an artist's chat has two kinds of work at the top. Pictures is the Draw form above, for every kind of picture. Character sets keeps the project's characters (a person, an animal, a mascot) and locations, so they look the same in every picture and shot. Each has a name, the words that describe it (always the same words), words to leave out, and its pictures. Make a sheet queues its views with one seed (front, three-quarter, side, back and a close-up for a character; wide, middle and close for a location); when they are drawn, press the good ones to add them, and tick up to 4 as references. Write @Name in What to draw, in a Videographer's clip or in a shot (or press its chip under What to draw), and its words are added each time. They are kept in the project's folder, under specialists\characters and specialists\locations, and shown on the project page. The chat must be in a project: in a chat with none, Make a project for these characters in the tab makes one and moves the chat into it. With Qwen-Image-Edit 2511 (up to 3) or FLUX.2 Klein 4B (up to 4) as the picture model, the ticked pictures of each @Name are given to it as references, characters first, so the face, markings and place stay the same; Full quality and Draw again use them too. Other picture models draw from the words alone. A picture drawn on a linked PC, or from the queue, uses the words only for now.
