Theme

Follow Windows, Day, Night or Auto; the Bridge's themes.

Settings, Theme picks the colours. They are kept in this browser, and every Help page follows them. The switch beside Settings at the top picks Day, Night or Auto too.

  • Follow Windows: light or dark, as Windows is set. This is what you get until you pick one.
  • Day: light grey and blue.
  • Night: dark grey and soft blue.
  • Auto: Day from sunrise to sunset, Night after. The sun is reckoned from this PC's time zone (its main city, or for a zone not listed, its offset and region), so it is close, not exact, and nothing is asked online.

The Bridge

The Bridge has its own themes, under its About page, Theme: Orbital is the space look, with the Orbit view of your running sites; Windows follows your Windows light or dark setting; Light and Dark. In Orbital, your running sites show as satellites over the Earth: click one to shoot it down (that stops it), and the arrow opens it.
