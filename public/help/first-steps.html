First steps

One PC first: a model, a hire, a project. Then your other PCs.

Start with one PC: put a model on it, hire someone to work on it, and keep what they make in a project. When that works, link your other PCs and spread the team across them. The pictures on this page were taken on a test copy with made-up staff, PCs and addresses.

The way through

  1. Install TOMLIN on this PC.
  2. Put a model on it, and see which one to pick for your PC.
  3. Hire someone to work on it, and talk to them.
  4. Keep what they make in a project.
  5. Link your other PCs, and move staff onto them.
  6. Back up your projects and your chats to those PCs.

Where to look for something

  • Pages you open from the left panel and Settings: Agents (standing jobs), Memory, Models, This PC, Network, Cluster, Data and lock, Chat permissions, Theme, Manage updates, Files and blog writer.
  • Projects in the left panel opens the Bridge: the project folders on this PC, git, audits and live sites.
  • Something wrong? The log and Report a problem.
