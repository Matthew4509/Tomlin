Copying models between PCs

Get a copy, send, move, and what a delete asks first.

Models are big, so keep them on more than one PC instead of downloading them again. On the Models page, To under each Download picks where it goes: This PC, or a linked PC that downloads it itself from the internet (nothing passes through this PC; not to a Backups only PC). Models on my other PCs shows how far it is, with Stop.

Models on my other PCs (Network, and the Models page) lists every model the linked PCs share: Get a copy from <PC> brings one here over your home network, so a model you deleted to save room comes back without downloading it again. A copy that was cut short carries on from where it stopped.

The other way, Send in the Move window, and Copy to <PC> in Hire staff's All models, put a model on a linked PC. Model runners on the Models page sends the NVIDIA (CUDA) runner to a linked PC with an NVIDIA card the same way (Send to): this PC downloads it once (checked), and that PC checks every file against the build its own TOMLIN names before it unpacks it. Remove from <PC> takes it away again.

A Backups only PC keeps projects and restore points, not models: to keep a spare copy of a model, keep it on another linked PC that shares it.

Before a model is deleted (the bin, or Free up space) the question says which staff work on it, as first choice or backup, and where else a copy is held (on which linked PCs, or "not checked" for a PC that is off or too old to say); Move to <PC> beside it copies it to a linked PC, checks the copy byte for byte, and only then removes it here. A hire whose model has gone says so in their chat and suggests a replacement (the same model on another PC to use or copy, or the nearest in size); nothing is copied, sent or switched until you press it.
