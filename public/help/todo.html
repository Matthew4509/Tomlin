To Do

Today, This week and This month, My day and quick checks.

Home's right panel, its third tab: what you mean to work on, in Today, This week and This month. Each row is a project (one of TOMLIN's, or a project folder on the Projects page), how long it takes (Quick, 15 minutes, or 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours) and a short note. + Add puts one in; on the Projects page a project's ⋯ menu has Add to To Do…; a recent project's card on Home drags onto a part.

Drag a row to another part, or use its ⋯ menu (move, note, quick check, remove). Tick a row when it is done: it goes to Done today. Nothing drops off by itself: a row not done today is still in Today tomorrow, marked with the day it came in.

The line at the top adds Today up against My day (8 hours to start) and says when it goes over.

A quick check (every week or every month) comes into Today by itself when it is due, 1 a day (2 if you pick it in My day), the longest waiting first. The To Do is kept in todo.json in your data folder.
