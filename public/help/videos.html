Making videos

The Videographer: video models that fit, short clips, the Videos shelf and clips on a linked PC.

A Videographer is a hire who makes short video clips from what you type. The clips are made with stable-diffusion.cpp and the Wan video models, on the graphics card of this PC or of a linked PC. A clip takes a long time: count in minutes or hours, not seconds.

Hire a Videographer

  1. Press Hire staff. Under Role, pick Pictures and video, then Video (Videographer) under Sub-role.
  2. Under Assign a PC, pick the PC that makes the clips: this PC, or a linked PC.
  3. Pick a video model. Each card shows the fits guide for that PC's graphics card: Fits (with 2 GB to spare), Tight fit (it only just fits) or Not enough memory. The line under it says what the model needs and what the card has. The needs are estimates until they are measured.
  4. A video model that is not downloaded on this PC cannot be picked yet. Press Get it on the Models page.

The video models

  • Wan 2.1 1.3B: the smallest. Short clips at 480p from a prompt. Needs about 8 GB of graphics memory and 16 GB of RAM.
  • Wan 2.2 5B Q8: packed to 8 bits so it fits a 12 GB card. Sharper than the 1.3B. Needs about 9 GB of graphics memory and 16 GB of RAM.
  • Wan 2.2 5B: full size, the best of the three, and a tight fit on a 12 GB card. Needs about 12 GB of graphics memory and 24 GB of RAM.

Download one from Models (in the Settings list): pick Video models in the list of kinds, then press Download. Each file is checked by SHA-256. The clips also need the picture and video runner for an NVIDIA card: it is under Model runners.

Make a clip

  • Open the Videographer's chat. Under the box you choose the Length (1 to 5 seconds), the Size (Wide 832×480, Tall 480×832 or Square 624×624) and the quality: Draft (a quick look, rougher motion), Normal (what the model is made for) or Best (more steps, slower).
  • Type what the clip should show and press Make clip (Enter does the same). Say what moves, for example "waves rolling onto a beach at sunset".
  • While it is made, the chat shows Clip in progress with a bar and a line: waiting, loading the video model, the frame it is on, then writing the clip file, with the time left.
  • Stop ends the clip. Nothing is kept.
  • You can open another chat meanwhile: the clip carries on, and its chat shows it again when you come back.
  • When it is done, the clip plays in the chat, with the PC it was made on and how long it took. Up to 5 clips can wait on one PC.

The Videos shelf

Every clip is on the Videos shelf, newest first. Open it with Videos under the chat box, or with Videos in the pictures gallery. Search the prompts to find an older clip. Delete asks once more before a clip goes for good. When there is under 2 GB free where clips are kept, the shelf and the chat say so, and no new clip is made until some space is freed.

A Videographer on a linked PC

  • Hire the Videographer on the linked PC under Assign a PC. The clip is made there, comes back over your home network, is checked, and is kept on this PC's Videos shelf.
  • That PC needs the video model and the NVIDIA picture and video runner. If it has neither, send them from here (below), or its owner downloads them from Models there.
  • The owner of that PC stays in charge. When its owner pressed I need to use the pc, it takes no new clips. Locked, it still makes them, as it draws pictures for linked PCs.

Send a video model or the runner to a linked PC

  • In Models, a downloaded video model has Send to: pick the PC and press Send … to that PC. Every file of the model goes, whole, and that PC checks each one by size and SHA-256. Network shows how far it is, with Stop.
  • The PC must have Accept models from linked PCs ticked and room for the model. A PC that cannot take it now says why in the list.
  • The NVIDIA picture and video runner is sent the same way, from its row under Model runners.

When a clip is not made

  • The video runner is not installed on this PC yet: download it under Models, Model runners, then ask again.
  • The video model for this hire is not on this PC: download it under Models, Video models, or pick another model in Edit staff.
  • The graphics card ran out of memory: try a shorter clip, a smaller size or Draft, or a smaller model.
  • There is under 2 GB free where clips are kept: free up some space, then ask again.
  • The video runner stopped by itself part way: ask again. If it keeps stopping, restart TOMLIN.
  • This PC is busy making other clips or 5 clips are already waiting: ask again once one of them is done.
  • Stopped: no clip was made: Stop was pressed.
  • Is being used by its owner for now: the linked PC's owner pressed "I need to use the pc". Try again later, or move the hire to another PC.
  • Is already making a clip or is busy: the linked PC is at other work. Try again when it is done.
  • Has no video runner installed: send the runner from here, or its owner installs it from Models there.
  • Runs an older TOMLIN that cannot make clips: update TOMLIN there.
  • Is off or not answering or stopped answering: the linked PC was turned off or left the network. No clip was kept. Pick a backup in the chat, or move the hire to another PC.
  • Changed on the way or is not a video: the clip that came back failed its check, so it was not kept. Ask again.

How long a clip takes

  • Z240, Quadro P2000 (5 GB): a short Wan 2.1 1.3B clip (a 5.1 MB WebM) took 84 minutes, with at most 4.7 GB of the card's memory in use.
  • Z820, RTX 3060 (12 GB): the same 3-second clip (832 by 480, Normal) took 11 minutes (10 min 48 s), asked from another PC over the link, and came back as a 1.2 MB WebM. It ran on the Vulkan runner; the faster CUDA one needs a newer NVIDIA driver there.

Measured with stable-diffusion.cpp, 25 November 2026. Longer, larger or Best clips take longer.
