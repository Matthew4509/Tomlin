Data and lock

Where your data is, the app lock PIN, restore points and notifications.

Settings, Data and lock: where your chats and staff are kept, how they are backed up, and who can open TOMLIN. It holds three parts: where things are kept, the lock, and notifications.

The app lock PIN

The app lock PIN (the padlock in the top bar; Settings) locks the whole app. It is also what a linked PC must type to update this one, turn it off or delete a model on it; with the same PIN on every PC of a cluster, Update all linked PCs asks for it once.

A host can instead give this PC its host PIN: one PIN for all its linked PCs. This PC takes it once, with its own PIN typed on the host, keeps only a scrambled copy, and takes it from that host only (never from another PC or a guest); the ticks under Sharing and cluster permissions are still needed. Network lists the hosts that hold one, under the PCs linked to this one, each with Take it away.

Wrong PINs typed on other PCs, this PC's own or a host PIN, are counted even across a restart: after 5 from one PC, it waits 10 minutes, then 1 hour, 6 hours and 24 hours each time after, until a right one.

Lost it? Close TOMLIN and delete app-lock.json from TOMLIN\data in your user folder: the PIN is gone and the chats stay.

Picture models: while TOMLIN is locked, a picture model you loaded is unloaded once it has had nothing to draw for 2 minutes (a linked PC drawing here loads one for its pictures, kept 10 minutes after its last one). While TOMLIN is open, a loaded picture model can be used by any program on this PC: its runner takes no key, so TOMLIN cannot lock it. Chat models take a key.

The padlock in the top bar locks the app at once; Lock by itself locks it after 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes without use, or never (only the padlock).

Backups and restore points

Your chats, staff and settings are backed up on this PC, and Put back brings one of those backups back. A restore point kept on another PC is brought into this PC's backups first: see Backups and restore points.

Web search

The search engine a chat with Web on uses is chosen here too: see Permissions and Web.

Notifications

Sounds and Windows notifications are set here, and can be muted for a while.
