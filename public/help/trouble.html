When something goes wrong

The log, and Report a problem.

TOMLIN writes what went wrong (a model that would not load, a chat or job step that stopped, a fault in the app) to a log file, one a day, the last 7 days kept, in TOMLIN\data\logs in your user folder. It holds where and what went wrong, never your chats or files, so it can be sent to someone who helps.

In the app, Help ends with Open the log (today's log file in Notepad, or the logs folder when today has none) and Report a problem. Report a problem makes a text on this PC and shows it whole; nothing is sent. Open on GitHub only opens the project's new-issue page in your browser, where you sign in and press Submit yourself.

The Bridge has its own list of faults: If something goes wrong in the Bridge. A link that will not make: Adding a PC, the Link helper.
